## How to use

1. Open the template in Google Sheets and make a personal copy (File → Make a copy).
2. Fill in the Seasonal Maintenance tab with your home’s regular tasks, selecting the appropriate category and frequency.
3. As you complete each task, enter the date in Last Completed; the Next Due column updates automatically.
4. When you incur a cost, log it in the Expense Tracker tab; totals and charts update in real time.
5. Review the Overview and Summary tabs each month to see upcoming tasks, overdue items, and your budget status.
