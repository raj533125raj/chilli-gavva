## How to use

1. Open the Google Sheet and go to the Settings tab; select your shop type (Etsy, Amazon Handmade, etc.) and set your default currency and shipping rate.
2. The Fees tab will auto‑populate the correct listing, transaction, and payment fee rates based on the shop type you chose.
3. Enter each product in the Profit tab: SKU, Units Sold, Unit Price, and Shipping Cost. The sheet will calculate all fees and show Net Profit per line item.
4. Use the Payments tab to log invoices, mark them Paid or Unpaid, and see which ones are overdue.
5. Check the Dashboard tab for a summarized view of total profit, average margin, and progress toward any profit goal you set.
