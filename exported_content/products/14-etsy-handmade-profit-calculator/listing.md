## Description

This ready‑to‑use Google Sheets workbook lets Etsy and other handmade shop owners instantly see how much they really earn after fees and shipping. Just plug in your sales data and the calculator does the heavy lifting—listing fees, transaction percentages, payment processor cuts, and shipping costs are all accounted for in real time.

All formulas are pre‑built and locked where appropriate, so you can focus on selling, not spreadsheet gymnastics. No tax advice is provided—this tool is purely for fee and profit calculation.

Designed for sellers worldwide, the sheet uses your chosen currency symbol and works in any locale that supports Google Sheets. Download, open in Google Drive, and start measuring true profitability today.
