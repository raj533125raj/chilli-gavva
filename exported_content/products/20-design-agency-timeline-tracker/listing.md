## Description

Keep your design studio organized with a lightweight, cloud‑based tracker that works in any browser. This Google Sheets template lets you list every project, break it down into deliverables, assign owners, and see at a glance which deadlines are approaching or overdue. No software downloads, no subscriptions—just a spreadsheet that updates in real time as your team logs hours.

The file includes a ready‑made Dashboard, a master Project list, a detailed Deliverables sheet, a time‑tracking Progress log, and an optional Finance ledger for quick income/expense monitoring. Built‑in formulas calculate days left, total hours, and completion percentages automatically, while conditional formatting flags risk areas so you can act fast.

Designed for small agencies, freelancers, and remote design teams worldwide, the template is fully customizable. Change colors, add columns, or connect the Progress tab to a Google Form for even smoother time entry. Start planning smarter today—download, copy, and hit “play” on your next project.
