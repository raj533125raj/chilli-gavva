## How to use

1. Duplicate the template into your own Notion workspace with a single click.
2. Add each of your enrolled courses in the Courses database; the semester tag will automatically appear on the Dashboard calendar.
3. For every homework, project, or exam, use the “New Assignment” or “New Exam” button – select the related course and fill the due date, weight, and any notes.
4. Update grades as you receive them; the Grade Tracker will recalculate your semester GPA instantly.
5. Review the Dashboard each morning to see today’s tasks, upcoming deadlines, and your current GPA at a glance.
