## Description

Stay on top of a full university semester with a single Notion workspace built specifically for students. This template gives you a clean Dashboard that pulls together every class, assignment due, and exam date on one calendar, so you never miss a deadline again.

Each course lives in its own database, linked to assignments, exams, and resources, letting you see everything related to a class in one place. The Assignment and Exam tables include status, priority, and weight fields, plus ready‑made views (calendar, list, Kanban) for quick planning.

The Grade Tracker uses formulas to turn your final grades and credit hours into an instant GPA calculation, updating automatically as you enter new scores. A dedicated Resources database lets you store PDFs, lecture links, and study notes right alongside the relevant course.

Designed for Notion’s free and paid plans, the template works on desktop, mobile, and web, making it ideal for students anywhere in the world. Simply duplicate, add your courses, and start tracking—no advanced Notion knowledge required.
