## How to use

1. Purchase the product on Gumroad and click the download link.
2. Make a copy of the Google Sheet into your own Google Drive.
3. Open the **Invoices** tab and start entering each invoice you issue (client, dates, amount, etc.).
4. When a payment arrives, change the **Status** to “Paid” and fill in the **Payment Date**; the row will disappear from the Overdue view automatically.
5. Review the **Overdue** tab daily to see which clients need follow‑up and check the **Summary** dashboard for a quick cash‑flow snapshot.
