## How to use

1. Purchase the product on Gumroad and download the ZIP file.
2. Extract the PDF files to a folder on your computer.
3. Print the pages on A4 or Letter paper (single‑sided or double‑sided as you prefer).
4. Cut or bind the pages if you want a physical planner, or keep the PDFs for digital use.
