## Description

Stay on top of stock, orders and supplier payments with a ready‑to‑use Google Sheets workbook designed for solo‑run e‑commerce stores.

- **Dashboard** gives you instant numbers: total SKUs, total stock value, how many items are below your reorder point, and how many orders are still pending.
- **Inventory** automatically calculates each product’s total value and flags any SKU that has slipped under its reorder quantity.
- **Orders** logs every sale, updates remaining stock in real time and warns you when an order can’t be fulfilled.
- **Payments** tracks what you owe suppliers, shows due dates and highlights overdue items so you never miss a payment.

All formulas, data‑validation and conditional formatting are pre‑built; just copy the file, paste your product data and start logging sales and payments. Because it lives in Google Sheets, you can access it from any device, share it with a teammate, and keep everything in one place.
