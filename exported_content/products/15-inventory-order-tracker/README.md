## How to use

1. Open the file in Google Sheets and choose **File → Make a copy** to add it to your Drive.
2. Fill in the **Inventory** tab with your SKUs, product names, current quantities, reorder thresholds and unit prices.
3. Whenever you make a sale, add a row to the **Orders** tab, select the appropriate status, and the sheet will update remaining stock and alert you to any over‑orders.
4. Record supplier invoices in the **Payments** tab; the sheet will highlight any unpaid invoices past their due date.
5. Watch the **Dashboard** tab for an at‑a‑glance view of stock health, pending orders and overdue payments.
