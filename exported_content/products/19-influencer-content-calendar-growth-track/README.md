## How to use

1. Open the Google Sheet and start with the “How‑to‑Use” tab for a full walkthrough.
2. In the Calendar tab, fill in each row with the date, platform, post type, caption, media URL, and set the Status to “Draft”.
3. When the post is ready, change Status to “Scheduled”. The “Ready?” column will flag posts that are due today or earlier.
4. After publishing, go to the Engagement Tracker tab and record Reach, Likes, Comments, Shares, and Saves. The sheet will auto‑calculate total interactions and engagement rate.
5. At month‑end, review the Overview and Forecast tabs to see your performance trends and projected growth for the next month.
