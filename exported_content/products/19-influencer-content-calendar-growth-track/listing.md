## Description

Plan, publish, and measure your social media content with a single, ready‑to‑use Google Sheet. The Calendar tab lets you schedule posts for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook, while the Status column helps you stay organized from draft to published.

After each post, log your metrics in the Engagement Tracker – the sheet automatically totals likes, comments, shares, and calculates an engagement rate, so you can see what resonates without manual math.

The Overview tab gives you a quick monthly dashboard, and the Forecast tab projects next‑month reach based on a growth percentage you set, helping you set realistic goals and track progress over time.

All tabs are pre‑formatted, include dropdowns and formulas, and come with a detailed How‑to‑Use guide, so you can start planning instantly—no spreadsheet expertise required.
