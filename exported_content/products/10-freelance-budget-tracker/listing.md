## Description

Stay on top of irregular pay‑checks with a ready‑to‑use Google Sheets budget built especially for freelancers and gig‑economy workers. The workbook separates income and expenses into dedicated tabs, automatically totals each month, and visualizes cash flow on a dashboard so you always know where your money is going.

* No coding or complex setup – just enter dates, amounts, and select a source or category.
* Real‑time charts show income vs. expenses and a breakdown of spending by category.
* Conditional formatting warns you when spending exceeds income or when invoices are overdue.
* Multi‑currency columns let you track earnings from different platforms without extra math.
* A step‑by‑step “How to Use” tab and a troubleshooting guide make onboarding fast, even if you’ve never used a spreadsheet for budgeting before.
