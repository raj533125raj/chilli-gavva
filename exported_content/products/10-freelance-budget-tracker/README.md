## How to use

1. Open the file in Google Sheets and choose **File → Make a copy** – this creates your own editable version while preserving the original as a reference.
2. In the **Overview** tab set the month (e.g., 2026‑03) and type your desired savings goal.
3. Enter every payment in the **Income** tab and every cost in the **Expenses** tab, using the drop‑down lists for Source and Category.
4. Return to **Overview** to see real‑time totals, net cash flow, and the two charts updating automatically.
5. Use the **Summary** tab to export a PDF report or copy the charts for personal reviews or to share with an accountant.
