## How to use

1. Add the product to your cart on Gumroad and click the “Get Google Sheets Template” button.
2. A copy of the file opens in your Google Drive – use File → Make a copy to save it to your own folder.
3. In the Overview tab, enter your semester name and the total amount you plan to spend on textbooks.
4. Fill in the Semester Planner with each course’s code, title, and start/end dates; the “Days Until Start” column updates automatically.
5. Open the Textbook List tab and add every required book, completing price fields and marking “Purchased?” when you buy a book. Totals update across the file instantly.
6. Check the Reports tab for visual charts that show where your money is going and whether you’re staying under budget.
