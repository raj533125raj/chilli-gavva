**Price:** 7.00 USD  
**Buyer:** College and university students who want a simple, visual way to plan, budget, and keep track of every textbook purchase each semester.

## Description

📚 All‑in‑one Google Sheets template built for college students to plan, track, and stay within textbook budgets each semester. The file lives in the cloud, so you can edit from any device, anywhere in the world.

✅ Instant insights – The Overview tab shows total budget, spending, and remaining funds at a glance, with colour alerts when you approach your limit.

🗓️ Semester‑wide organization – Use the Semester Planner to map courses and dates, then list every required textbook with price, store, and purchase status. Automatic formulas calculate totals and percentages—no manual summing needed.

📊 Ready‑made reports – The Reports tab generates charts that visualise spend by category and week‑by‑week trends, helping you spot patterns without building graphs yourself.
