# Printable Focus Sheets Pack

## What's inside

- `printable-focus-sheets-A4.pdf`
- `printable-focus-sheets-Letter.pdf`
- `listing.md`
- `cover_image_idea.txt`

## How to use

1. Download the PDF file to your computer or tablet.
2. Print the pages at 100% scale on standard US Letter or A4 paper, or open the file in a PDF annotation app.
3. Start with the Brain Dump page whenever you feel overwhelmed, then move your top three items to the Daily Focus page.

## Notes

- This is a planning and organising tool for personal use. It does not give financial, medical, legal or career advice and does not promise any result.
- Sample rows and placeholder text are examples: replace them with your own.
