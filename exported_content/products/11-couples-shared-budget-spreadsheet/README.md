## How to use

1. Make a copy of the Google Sheet to your own Google Drive (File → Make a copy).
2. Enter your joint accounts in the Overview tab and link them to the Income and Expenses tabs.
3. Record all income entries in the Income tab and every bill or purchase in the Expenses tab, selecting who paid it and setting a due date if applicable.
4. Review the Overview tab regularly to see current balances, total income, total expenses, and net balance.
