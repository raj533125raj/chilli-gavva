## Description

Manage joint finances with ease using this ready‑to‑use Google Sheets template. Designed for couples living together, traveling, or sharing a household, the spreadsheet lets you log income, split bills, and monitor savings goals—all in one place. No formulas to write—everything is pre‑built and updates automatically.

The file includes three core tabs: an Overview that shows balances across all accounts, an Income log to capture every source of money, and an Expenses tracker that records each spend, who paid it, and when it’s due. Conditional formatting highlights overdue payments and overspending, while built‑in formulas keep a running balance for each partner.

Because it’s a Google Sheet, you can access it from any device—desktop, tablet, or phone—anywhere in the world. Share the sheet with your partner, set edit permissions, and collaborate in real time. Start budgeting together today, without expensive apps or complicated software.
