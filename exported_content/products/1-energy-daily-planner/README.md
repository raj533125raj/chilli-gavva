# Energy-Based Daily Planner

## What's inside

- `energy-daily-planner-A4.pdf`
- `energy-daily-planner-Letter.pdf`
- `listing.md`
- `cover_image_idea.txt`

## How to use

1. Download the PDF file to your computer or tablet.
2. Print the daily sheet or low-energy sheet on standard letter or A4 paper.
3. At the start of your day, assess your energy level and pick tasks that fit your current capacity.

## Notes

- This is a planning and organising tool for personal use. It does not give financial, medical, legal or career advice and does not promise any result.
- Sample rows and placeholder text are examples: replace them with your own.
