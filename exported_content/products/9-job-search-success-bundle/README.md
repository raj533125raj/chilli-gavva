## How to use

1. Download the zip file from Gumroad and unzip it.
2. Open the Resume Template PDFs in Word or Google Docs, replace the placeholder text with your own information, and export as PDF when finished.
3. Open the Google Sheets tracker (or import the .xlsx file into Google Sheets) and copy the example rows to start entering each job you apply to.
4. Use the Follow‑Ups and Interviews tabs to schedule reminders and record notes; the date fields can be linked to Google Calendar via simple formulas if you wish.
5. Refer to the Quick‑Start Guide whenever you need a refresher on editing or customizing the bundle.

## Notes

- This is a planning and organising tool for personal use. It does not give financial, medical, legal or career advice and does not promise any result.
- Sample rows and placeholder text are examples: replace them with your own.
