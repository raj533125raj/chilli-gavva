# Printable Time-Blindness Planner - Today Page, Weekly Overview and Monthly Review

**Price:** 5.00 USD  
**Buyer:** neurodivergent adults who like simple printable planners

## Description

A printable planner for people who lose track of time. It plans your day by anchors, not by the clock.

The Today page has time anchors, a Top 3 (only 3), Now / Next / Later, waiting for / errands, wins today, a guess vs real time table, sixteen 15-minute focus blocks, tomorrow's first step and a brain dump.

Also inside: a cover, a How to use page, the Weekly Overview page, the Monthly Review page and the Notes & Reflections page. Print it on A4 or US Letter paper, as often as you need, for personal use.
