## How to use

1. Open the Debts tab and enter every outstanding loan or credit‑card balance with its interest rate and minimum payment.
2. Whenever you make a payment, log it in the Payments tab (date, debt name, amount, optional note).
3. Switch to the Progress Chart tab to view a live chart of remaining balances and how close each debt is to its target payoff date.
4. Adjust the Target Payment column in the Debts tab to experiment with faster payoff schedules; the sheet recalculates automatically.
5. Refer to the How to Use tab for troubleshooting and tips on keeping the tracker up‑to‑date.
